About Us

Pioneering aviation lighting solutions since the early days

Our Story

With a background of over nineteen years of experience, Shaham Electronics Aviation is a recognized leader in the design development and production for adaptation of cockpits to night vision goggles (NVG) generation III.

As an Israeli Air Force Approved Supplier, we have built our reputation on delivering reliable, high-quality aviation lighting solutions that meet the most demanding military specifications.

Our expertise spans the complete range of cockpit illumination systems, from initial assessment and design through manufacturing and integration.

Our Approach

Our main concept for integration and adaptation is low cost with minimum changes, easy to install with unique solutions, using the standard maintenance procedure.

We understand that aircraft operators need solutions that can be implemented efficiently without extensive downtime or complex modifications to existing systems.

This philosophy has guided our product development and earned us the trust of military and commercial aviation customers worldwide.

Our Capabilities

Shaham Electronics Aviation has the capability of a complete NVG cockpit adaptation, including light balancing and measurements, as well as providing solutions to prevent reflection and interferences in the cockpit.

Our experience in the field of aircraft adaptation for NVG compatibility is in part, as a result of our expertise in designing and manufacturing all types of:

  • • Light plates
  • • Edgelight panels
  • • Control boxes
  • • Flat displays
  • • LED modules lights
